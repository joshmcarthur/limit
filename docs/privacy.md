# Limit privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Limit is made in New Zealand by Josh McArthur. It doesn't collect your personal information. There's no account and no analytics, and your location stays on your phone.

## Location

Limit asks for your location while you use the app, so it can find the road you're on and measure your speed. It's used on your phone as you drive and isn't saved, shared or sent anywhere.

Once you start a drive, Limit keeps using your location while your phone is locked or the app is in the background, so the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island stay current. iOS shows a blue indicator while this happens. It stops when you tap "End drive".

## What Limit keeps on your phone

Your settings, the downloaded speed limit register, and a note of when Limit last checked for updates. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## What Limit sends

The only thing Limit sends over the internet is the request for the speed limit register, from a public service run for Waka Kotahi NZ Transport Agency. The request names the app and its version. As with any request on the internet, the service can see your phone's IP address. It doesn't include your location, and I don't run that service or receive anything from it.

## What Limit doesn't have

No account, advertising, tracking or analytics, and no code from other companies. If you've chosen to share iPhone analytics with developers, Apple may pass me crash reports. They don't identify you.

## Children

Limit isn't aimed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

## Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information an agency holds about you. Limit holds none about you, so there's nothing to access or correct. Questions: limit-app@joshmcarthur.com. You can also contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz).

If this changes, I'll update the date above.
